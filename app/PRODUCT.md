# Product System

## Positioning

Aurelium Field replaces the fragmented contractor stack with one calm workspace spanning sales, estimating, field documentation, production, people, project controls and customer communication. Painting is a first-class trade model rather than a generic note field.

## Primary roles

- Owner / administrator
- Estimator / salesperson
- Project manager
- Foreman
- Crew member
- Office / accounting
- Client / owner representative

## Core lifecycle

Lead → walkthrough → estimate → proposal → acceptance → scheduling → production → field documentation → changes → completion → closeout → follow-up.

## Product pillars

1. **Smart Estimate** — RoomPlan/LiDAR + narration + photos + production rates + human verification.
2. **Projects** — scope, schedule, documents, photos, tasks, changes, daily logs and financial health.
3. **Field** — deliberately fast capture for photos, time, logs, punch, safety and documents.
4. **People** — scheduling, skills, availability, timecards and labor budgets.
5. **CRM** — contacts, opportunities, follow-up, proposal activity and job conversion.
6. **Painting Production** — walls/ceilings/trim/doors/cabinets, prep classes, coats, products, coverage, labor production and material yield.
7. **Communication** — project threads, mentions, approvals and client-facing updates without making persistent content dependent on online presence.

## Smart Estimate measurement rules

- Device-derived geometry is never represented as guaranteed exact.
- Manual overrides are preserved with an audit record.
- Openings can be deducted or retained according to company estimating policy.
- Surface areas and linear footage are derived deterministically from captured dimensions.
- AI can classify likely scope and summarize observations, but cannot silently mutate authoritative quantities.
- Unsupported/no-LiDAR devices fall back to guided manual measurements and photo/narration capture.

## Painting data model expansion

Production-rate metadata is reserved for coating-system details such as substrate, condition, prep class, application method, coats, product, spread rate, waste percentage, labor setup, mask/protect allowances, mobilization, minimum charges and crew productivity.
