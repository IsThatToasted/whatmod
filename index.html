04 / GOOD TO KNOW

Questions? Covered.

Do I have to pay to try a tool?

No. Each tool can offer a free tier or preview. Premium feature availability is shown on its detail page.

How does the unlock work?

After successful checkout, your unique license key appears on the confirmation page. Copy and keep it. Supported applications can validate that key against the WhatMod license service.

Does one purchase unlock everything?

Your supporter key unlocks premium features in the participating WhatMod seller tools listed as eligible. Third-party services, future standalone paid products, and tools marked otherwise are not included.

Is WhatMod affiliated with Whatnot?

No. WhatMod is an independent project and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Whatnot. Use tools responsibly and follow platform policies.
